A Brief History of Mars Exploration

From NASA's Mariner 4 to the Curiosity Rover

The first successful flyby of Mars was achieved by NASA's Mariner 4 in 1964.

Since then, numerous robotic missions have been sent to the Red Planet.

  • Mariner 4 (1964)
  • Viking 1 and 2 (1975)
  • Mars Science Laboratory (2011)
